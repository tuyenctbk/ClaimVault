Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Welcome to ClaimVault ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

ClaimVault is designed with a strong focus on privacy and local data storage (offline-first architecture):

  • User-Provided Data: Warranty details, receipt images, categories, notes, purchase dates, and store information that you explicitly input into the app.
  • Device Permissions: Camera access (only when you choose to snap photos of receipts or warranty cards) and storage access (to save or export PDF warranty reports).

2. How We Store Your Data

All your warranty records, receipts, and personal data are stored locally on your device using secure local database storage (Room/SQLite). We do not operate external remote servers that collect or store your personal receipts and warranties without your explicit action or backup integration.

3. Use of Information

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Manage and organize your warranties and receipts.
  • Send you local push notifications/alerts regarding upcoming warranty expiration dates.
  • Generate PDF summaries and claim exports for your personal use.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data stays on your device.

5. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Because your data is stored locally on your device, you are also encouraged to secure your device with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@claimvault.app.
